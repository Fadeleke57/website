- Short blog in reference to this [tweet](https://x.com/xeophon/status/2010358108212605397?s=10).
- The last mile of ai engineering are an optimization problem: https://x.com/fchollet/status/2024519439140737442
- Way easier to finish the last mile when you've been set up from the start.
- Labs hill climbing on evals means you should format your prompts and problems as things that can be measured and optimized.
- Measurement leads to lead good evals 
- Good evals leads to potentially good datasets to pass into your next optimization run (as a prompt optimizer, SFT, RL etc.
